# r-aloud privacy policy

r-aloud does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **Text you ask it to read** (web pages, selections, PDFs) is sent only to the
  r-aloud text-to-speech server running on your own computer
  (`http://127.0.0.1:8771` by default, or another address you configure). It is
  never sent to the extension's developer or any third party.
- **Local PDFs** you open in the r-aloud reader are read from your disk, either
  by the browser or by the local server, and stay on your machine.
- **Settings** (engine, voice, speed, server address) are stored in your
  browser's extension storage and never leave it.
- There is **no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking** of any kind.

Questions: https://github.com/AvAkanksh/r-aloud/issues
