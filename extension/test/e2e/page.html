The quiet revolution of open speech models

Speech synthesis used to be robotic. Today, small open models sound remarkably human.

Kokoro is one such model. It has only eighty two million parameters, yet it reads long articles with natural rhythm and clear pronunciation.

Larger systems like MOSS TTS Realtime go further. They model conversational context, clone voices from a short sample, and stream audio while text is still arriving.

Running these models locally means your reading habits stay private. Nothing leaves your machine.
